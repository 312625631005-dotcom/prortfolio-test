// SELECTED PRODUCTS

What Perplexity actually ships

Not side projects — the company's real product line, built and shipped under his direction since 2022.

Perplexity
CORE PRODUCT

The original answer engine: it takes a question in plain language and returns a direct, synthesized answer with in-line citations back to its sources — instead of a page of links to click through yourself.

SINCE 2022Try it
Comet
BROWSER

A Chromium-based browser with an AI sidebar built in. It can summarize pages, compare prices, draft emails, and carry out multi-step tasks across open tabs — released free to everyone in October 2025.

LAUNCHED 2025Try it
Comet Assistant
AGENT

An agent that lives inside Comet and acts on a page rather than just reading it — managing tabs, triaging email, scheduling, and completing routine multi-step workflows on a person's behalf.

IN COMETLearn more
Deep Research
RESEARCH MODE

An asynchronous mode that runs a longer, multi-step investigation across many sources on its own, then returns one cited report — the shift from single-turn answers toward a research platform.

Where the research went

His PhD work wasn't abstract — each area maps onto something Perplexity actually runs on today.

TRANSFORMERS
Language & generation models
The architecture behind every large language model Perplexity orchestrates → the core answer-generation engine.
CONTRASTIVE LEARNING
Representation & retrieval
Learning what makes two pieces of content related → ranking and matching sources to a query.
REINFORCEMENT LEARNING
Policy & decision-making
Optimizing a sequence of actions toward an outcome → agents like Comet Assistant deciding what to do next.
VISION-LANGUAGE MODELS
Multimodal understanding
Connecting images and text in one model → answering questions that mix screenshots, pages, and prose.
